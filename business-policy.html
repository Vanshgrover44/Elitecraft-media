1. Business Overview

EliteCraft Media is presented on its website as a one-person creative studio operated by Rehaan. The studio works directly with founders, creators and growing brands, providing creative, web and growth services without agency layers or account managers.

2. Services

Current service categories shown on the website are:

  • Website Design & Development
  • Logo & Brand Identity
  • SEO & Search Visibility
  • Digital Marketing
  • Magazine & Editorial Design
  • Graphic Design & Collateral

3. Pricing

The website presents fixed package prices in USD, including Logo & Identity at $95, Website Design at $300, and Magazine Layout at $180. The website also states that prices may vary depending on design complexity, number of pages and quantity, and that an exact quote should be obtained for a specific project.

4. Payment & Project Start

A 50% advance is collected upfront via PayPal to begin work, with the remaining balance due before final files are delivered. Project work begins after the required advance payment and necessary client information are received.

Advance payment: Payment reserves project time and authorises EliteCraft Media to commence the agreed work.

5. Timelines & Dependencies

The website states that most projects take approximately 4 days to 2 weeks. Timelines depend on the service, scope, content availability and client response time.

Delays caused by missing content, images, files, account access, approvals, corrections or feedback may extend the project timeline. A timeline should therefore be treated as a working estimate unless a fixed date has been expressly confirmed in writing.

6. Communication & Client Cooperation

EliteCraft Media follows a direct 1:1 working model. Clients are expected to provide accurate information, required files and timely feedback. The client should identify all material corrections within the included revision rounds and avoid sending fragmented or conflicting instructions.

7. Revisions & Additional Work

Two structured revision rounds are included in every package according to the website. Additional rounds can be added at a fixed rate. Work that materially changes the original scope, structure, number of pages, deliverables or creative direction may require an additional quotation.

8. Delivery & Handover

Final files, source assets and a walkthrough are provided where applicable and included in the agreed package. Final ownership transfers after the final payment has been received, as stated in the website FAQ.

9. Digital Services

EliteCraft Media primarily provides digital/electronic services. Deliverables may include websites, digital graphics, logos, PDFs, editorial layouts, source files and other electronic assets. No physical goods are supplied unless specifically agreed in writing for a particular project.
