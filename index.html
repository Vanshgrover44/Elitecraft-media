08 — FAQ

Things people usually ask.

How long does a project take?

+

Most logos take 4–6 days, websites 1–2 weeks, and magazine layouts 1–2 weeks depending on page count.

How does payment work?

+

A 50% advance is collected upfront via PayPal to begin work, with the remaining balance due before final files are delivered.

How many revisions do I get?

+

Every package includes two structured revision rounds. Additional rounds can be added at a fixed rate.

What files do I receive?

+

You get final production files (PNG, PDF, or live site handover) plus editable source files where applicable.

Who owns the final design?

+

Full ownership transfers to you once the final payment is received.
